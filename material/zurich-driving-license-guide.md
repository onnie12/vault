# Getting a Category B Driving License in Zürich

## The full process, in order

1. **Nothelferkurs (first aid course)**
   Required before you can apply for a learner's permit, unless you already hold a category A, A1, B or B1 license. Must be with a recognized provider (Samariterbund, Red Cross, or private providers like nohe.ch, BLINK, etc.).
   - Duration: 10 hours, split across at least two days.
   - No exam at the end. Attend all the hours, don't cause problems, and you get the certificate.
   - You get a certificate (Nothilfekurs/Nothelferausweis) from the provider, either on paper or digitally through their portal. Valid 6 years from the course date.
   - You attach this certificate to your Lernfahrgesuch when you submit it (not stamped onto the form like the Sehtest). Sources disagree on whether the original or a copy is required, check the exact wording on the form or ask the Strassenverkehrsamt.
   - What matters for the 6-year window is the date you *submit* the Lernfahrgesuch, not the date of your practical exam later on.
   - You can take and finish it before your 17th birthday.

2. **Sehtest (eye test)**
   - Must be done at a Swiss optician or doctor. A glasses prescription visit does **not** count, it has to be the specific standardized vision test.
   - Print the Lernfahrgesuch form first and bring it to the appointment. The optician fills in the result directly on the form (date, signature, stamp).
   - Valid 2 years (24 months) from the test date to the date you submit your application.
   - Costs around CHF 20.

3. **Lernfahrgesuch (learner's permit application)**
   Submitted to the Strassenverkehrsamt Kanton Zürich, with:
   - A passport photo
   - Your Nothelfer certificate
   - The Sehtest filled in on the form
   - If this is your first application, you also need to confirm your identity in person, either at your local Einwohnerkontrolle or at the Strassenverkehrsamt.
   - You can submit this a month before you turn 17.

4. **Theorieprüfung (theory exam)**
   - After your application is approved, you get exam registration details by mail.
   - Computer-based, 45 minutes, maximum 15 error points out of 150.
   - Offered in German, French, Italian and English in Zürich.
   - Fee is commonly quoted around CHF 30 to 40, but that figure comes from a driving-school blog, not the cantonal site. Check stva.zh.ch for the confirmed number.

5. **Lernfahrausweis issued**
   - Valid 24 months for category B, cannot be extended.
   - You can only apply for a given category twice.

6. **Driving practice (Lernfahrten)**
   - With a driving instructor, or a private accompanying person who is at least 23, has held an unrestricted category B license (not on probation) for at least 3 years, is at 0.0 alcohol, and sits in the passenger seat.
   - If you get your learner's permit before turning 20, you must complete a minimum 12-month learning phase before the practical exam. If you're already 20+, that minimum doesn't apply.

7. **VKU (Verkehrskundeunterricht, traffic awareness course)**
   - 8 hours total, split into two 4-hour blocks.
   - Done after you have your learner's permit, mandatory before you can register for the practical exam.
   - Cost commonly quoted around CHF 180 to 280 depending on the provider, confirm with whoever you book through.
   - Heads up: a new regulation (VKUV) reportedly changes the VKU format starting 2027. Details weren't verifiable, so if your timeline stretches into next year, check with the Strassenverkehrsamt or a driving school.

8. **Praktische Fahrprüfung (practical driving test)**
   - Minimum age 18.
   - Only after the 12-month rule (if it applies to you) and the VKU are done.

9. **Führerausweis auf Probe (probationary license)**
   - Issued once you pass, valid for a 3-year probation period.

10. **WAB course (2-Phasen-Ausbildung)**
    - Mandatory one-day, 7-hour further training course, must be attended within the first 12 months of the 3-year probation period.

11. **Unbefristeter Führerausweis (permanent license)**
    - Sent automatically shortly before the probation period ends, once the WAB course is done.

## Still open

- Whether the 12-month learning phase (step 6) applies to you depends on your age when you get the learner's permit. Worth confirming for yourself.
- Whether the Nothelferausweis needs to be submitted as an original or a copy, sources disagreed.
